# Reinsurance Collateral & Trust Funding Reconciliation

Calculate required collateral and prevent excess funding, shortfalls, and delayed trust or letter-of-credit releases.

**Primary buyer:** Cedents, reinsurers, and captive managers. **Evidence:** reinsurance agreements, reserves, paid losses, recoverables, letters of credit, trust assets, haircuts, ratings, collateral calls, and releases.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Agreement collateral terms
- Reserve recoverable ingestion
- Paid loss recoverables
- Required collateral calculation
- Asset eligibility review
- Valuation haircut calculation
- Credit rating adjustment
- Letter-of-credit tracking
- Trust statement reconciliation
- Funds-withheld balance
- Collateral call workflow
- Excess release calculation
- Counterparty dispute evidence
- Accounting reconciliation
- Treaty counterparty analytics

Run `./start.sh`, then open <http://127.0.0.1:4662>. API: `5662`.

Administrator: `runtime-admin@example.com` / `LocalDemo!2026`. Operator and reviewer credential buttons are available on the login page.
